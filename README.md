# Olist E-Commerce Analytics & Performance Project

## Executive Summary
This project presents an end-to-end data analytics solution for Olist, the largest E-commerce department store marketplace in Brazil. By analyzing multi-thousand order records spanning across 2016 to 2018, the dashboard delivers actionable executive insights into regional sales distributions, delivery SLAs, and key satisfaction drivers using AI-powered visuals.

View Project Portfolio & Visuals on Maven Analytics: https://mavenshowcase.com/project/57857

## Technical Architecture & Tools
* **Database Management & Extraction:** SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)
* **ETL & Data Transformation:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema Design (Handling Role-Playing Dimensions for Customers & Sellers)
* **Visualization & AI Analytics:** Power BI Desktop (DAX, Decomposition Tree, Key Influencers, Custom Tooltips)

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Business Insights
* **Revenue & Volume:** Generated **$13.59M** in overall sales across **99K** orders with an Average Order Value (AOV) of **$136.68**.
* **Logistics Performance:** Overall Average Delivery Time stands at **12.50 days** with an overall Late Delivery Rate of **8.11%**.
* **Friction Hotspots:** Order delays are heavily concentrated in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro metropolitan hubs.
* **Payment Preference:** Credit Card dominates payment choices, driving **78.34%** of overall transaction volume ($16.01M).
* **Customer Rating Driver (AI Analysis):** Delivery delay (`Is Late = 1`) is identified as the single primary factor dropping review scores by an average of **1.55 stars** (reducing scores from 4.21 down to 2.66).

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Regional Warehouse Expansion:** Establish fulfillment centers or partner with local 3PL providers near São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro to shorten delivery times and eliminate the 8.11% delay rate.
2. **Dispatch SLAs:** Enforce stricter seller dispatch SLAs in high-delay zones to protect customer satisfaction ratings.
3. **Logistics-First Strategy:** Focus capital on dispatch speed rather than shipping subsidies, as AI analysis proves freight costs have zero impact on customer review scores.
